# Food Price Explorer: plan

Interactive Power BI dashboard on staple food prices in Nigeria (WFP market data),
focused on the North East, with a Nigeria vs Niger comparison.

## Data
- `data/raw/wfp_food_prices_nga.csv`: WFP Nigeria market prices, 2002 to Sep 2026 (HDX)
- `data/raw/wfp_food_prices_ner.csv`: WFP Niger market prices, 1990 to Aug 2026 (HDX)

Coverage note: WFP monitors Nigerian markets mainly in the North East (Borno, Yobe, Adamawa),
where its humanitarian operations are. North Central and South South have no data; North West,
South West and South East stop in Jan 2023. The dashboard therefore focuses on the North East.

2. **North East markets: where is food most expensive?**
   - [ ] Map of markets coloured by price / price change

3. **Nigeria vs Niger: is it the food or the currency?**

4. **Data coverage**
   - [ ] Markets per state / commodity / year, gaps, and the zone coverage table
- [ ] Hidden tooltip page (mini price trend on map hover)

## Decisions from data profiling
- Staples tracked through the crisis (North East retail, monitored 2020-2026): rice (local), rice (imported),
  beans, cowpeas, millet, sorghum, yam, maize flour, palm oil, plus tomatoes, onions, sugar, eggs, beef.
- Plain maize and gari are only monitored until 2023 in the North East, so they appear in history only.
- Nigeria vs Niger uses the staples both countries monitor through 2026: millet, sorghum, rice (imported), cowpeas.
- USD prices use the official exchange rate WFP applies (implied rates saved in `fx_monthly.csv`).
- 333 records (0.2%) are flagged as outliers (>5x or <0.2x the monthly median for the same food); flagged, not removed.
- "Aggregate" records are monthly averages of weekly collection; no duplicates, so all are kept.
